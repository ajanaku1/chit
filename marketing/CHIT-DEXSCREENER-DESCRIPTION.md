Chit funds the fleet instead, so that link never gets drawn.

The bigger piece is the money you actually trade with. That comes from a shared pool your fleet draws against instead of from your main wallet. Fixed deposit sizes, caps kept deliberately small, and an exit you can take yourself after 24 hours if we don't pay out. It is written and passing its tests. It is not deployed, so do not read it as something you can use yet.

Private, not anonymous. Your trades still show, and so do the amounts and the timing. We can see the mapping ourselves, and we would rather say so than let you find out. Your keys stay yours.

CHIT gets you access and discounts. You never need it to use Chit.

Testnet, test tokens, no audit. chit.tools

## Why it reads this way

The pool is in future tense throughout, because it is not deployed. The only thing claimed as working is gas sponsorship, and there is a transaction behind that.

"Private, not anonymous" is in there because on a token page the word private on its own gets read as anonymous by anyone skimming.

The CHIT line keeps a funding-privacy product from being read as a price thesis. No returns, no dates, no audit implied.

Update it when the pool deploys. The pool paragraph moves up into the working paragraph then, with its own transaction hash.
