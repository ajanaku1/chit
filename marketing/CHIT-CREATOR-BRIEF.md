# Chit video brief

**For:** the creator making the Chit video.
**From:** the Chit team · 7 Sep 2026
**Length:** one page. Read the whole thing, the second half is the part that keeps us both out of trouble.

---

## What Chit is, in two lines

If you trade with a fleet of wallets, funding them all from one main wallet draws a map straight back to you. Chit funds the fleet instead, so that map never gets drawn.

That is the whole pitch. Everything else is detail.

---

## The one visual that does the work

Open a bubble map and show 30 wallets radiating out from a single funding wallet. Say "this is you."

Ten seconds of that beats any amount of explanation. The problem is visible, which is rare in this category, so spend your opening there and get to the product second. Most videos about privacy tools start with the tool. Please do not.

---

## Three things worth highlighting

The trail is the product. Chit breaks the funding link between your main wallet and your fleet. Not the trades. Those stay public, along with the amounts and the timing. It is the funding trail that clusters a fleet back to one person, and that is the thing being cut.

Private, not anonymous. Chit can still see the mapping in this version, and we say so out loud. That sentence is the most useful thing you can put in the video. It separates us from every mixer-adjacent project going, and a limit sounds different coming from you than it does from us.

You can leave without asking. Deposits sit in a shared pool with small caps on purpose, and the contract has an exit you can take yourself after 24 hours if we never pay out. A pool always raises "so they are holding my money?" Answer it in the video, before the comments do.

---

## What you can say, and what you cannot

Say:

- "Private funding." "It breaks the trail that clusters your fleet back to you."
- "You keep your keys. Chit never holds them."
- "The team says the operator can still see the mapping." Disclosing this is a feature, not a caveat.
- "Live on testnet." "Hardened contracts, tested against a live chain."

Do not say:

- Anonymous. Untraceable. Invisible. Mixer. Anything in that family.
- "Your trades are private." They are not, and claiming it is the fastest way to lose the audience that knows better.
- "Audited." There is no third-party audit yet.
- Live now, trade on Chit today, or anything implying real money. It is testnet with a test token.
- Anything about price, returns, or $CHIT going up. Hard rule, legal as much as reputational. $CHIT is optional access and discounts, never required to use the product.

If a claim would leave someone thinking their trades are hidden or that they are anonymous, it is wrong. When unsure, downgrade it or ask.

---

## Two things to avoid on screen

Skip the swirling-funds animation. Pooled money tends to get drawn as a tumbler, which is Tornado Cash's visual language, and that association is genuinely dangerous for a privacy product. Draw the pool as a shared balance sheet or a queue instead.

Skip the call to action. There is no link to send people to yet. It is testnet only, early access has not opened, and a "link in bio" either sends people somewhere they cannot use or gets someone trying to deposit real ETH. End on follow and watch this get built.

## Timing

The pool that funds the whole trade is being built now and is not live yet, so there is nothing working to film for that part.

That is fine, because the problem explainer does not depend on it. Shoot that now, it stays relevant for months. Then when the first pooled trade lands on chain, we hand you the transaction hash and you do a short second video with the proof in it. Two pieces of content instead of one, and the second one has receipts.
