# Chit art direction

## Signature element — Blind seam

A single physical chit is divided by a precise zigzag seam. Both sides stay public, but the path
across the seam cannot be followed. The torn-ticket silhouette grounds the mark in the product name.

## Material and composition

Flat, cut-paper geometry with a high-contrast silhouette. A rounded ink tile contains one signal
coral chit divided by an ink-colored blind seam. Chamfered outer corners keep it physical rather than
diagrammatic. The icon is centered with a consistent outer margin. There are no outlines, shadows,
gradients, or decorative texture.

## Palette

- Signal coral: `#FF5A3C`
- Paper: `#F5EFE5`
- Ink: `#171513`

Signal coral is the only accent. All other product colors are neutral.

## Tiny-size target

The blind seam must remain visible at 16px without turning the two sides into unrelated shapes. At
larger sizes its paired notches should reward a second look.

## Forbidden

No word or initial inside the icon. No coin, receipt outline, gas pump, padlock, shield, eye,
fingerprint, chain link, hexagonal network, node graph, cube, sparkle, glow, rainbow, or extra motif.
