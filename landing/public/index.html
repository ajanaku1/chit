One bounded sponsorship campaign, drawn across five accounts.

05 / privacy boundary

Public endpoints. A private relationship.

Public record
Smart accounts, trades, amounts, timing, gas, sponsor participation, and sponsorship activity are visible.
Private attribution
The public chain does not publish the primary-wallet-to-fleet relationship.
Operator boundary
The service operator knows the mapping and the relationship. Chit does not claim operator blindness or promise that observers cannot connect every public signal.
How the funding stays apart
Your ETH goes into a shared pool that carries no fleet marker, and Chit funds fleets from that pool, so no transaction links the two. Amounts and timing can still line up while the pool is small, and we say so rather than pretend otherwise.
Private, but not anonymous
Chit is not a mixer and does not try to make anyone disappear. The public cannot join your wallet to your fleet; the operator can. The direction is a key you hold, so you decide who else ever can.

06 / control and risk

A fleet should have a short leash.

Pause

Stops new sponsored execution. Only a paused campaign may resume.

Revoke

Ends sponsorship for the campaign. Revocation is terminal; a new campaign is required to continue.

Close

Returns unused campaign ETH and blocks further sponsored execution.
