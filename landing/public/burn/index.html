the rule

10% of the fleet fees, plus one point for every 100k of market cap

the team sends that share of the fees into the contract every day. the mcap is read when the day's deposit is posted, so the share follows the coin: the bigger chit gets, the bigger the bite that gets burned.

the share is the team's rule and the daily post shows what actually arrived; the contract itself only knows eth in and chit out. market cap here is dexscreener's, named as such.

how it works

a contract with no owner and no withdraw

four steps, every one of them on the chain. a keeper only decides when; what happens is fixed in the code.

01

eth goes in

anyone can fund it. the team's daily share lands here by plain transfer, and every deposit is an event.

02

once an hour, a buy

1% of what it holds a call, floor 0.002 eth, cap 0.1 eth. anyone can call it; a keeper does when it is due.

03

bought on the pool

through the universal router on the chit/eth v4 pool. it refuses a fill more than 5% under the pool's own quote.

04

burned, same transaction

every chit it receives goes to the dead address before the call returns. it cannot hold chit and it cannot send eth anywhere but the pool.

the contract

read it, verify it, call it

the parameters are immutable: a different number would be a different contract. the deployer's key is worth nothing after the deploy.
