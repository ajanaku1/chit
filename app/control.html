What a pause does

Deposits stop and draws stop. Exits keep working: anyone who has asked to leave can still take their money out, which is why the exit lives in the contract rather than in a promise. The pool is stopped, not stuck.

Only the admin can resume, and only after the resume gate passes and an account of what happened is published. Pausing is cheap and reversible; resuming is neither.
