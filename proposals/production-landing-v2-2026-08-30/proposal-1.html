Chit is building private gas sponsorship for one trader operating 5–50 newly generated programmable smart accounts.

See the operating line ↓

01 / mechanism

One bounded line of sponsorship.

FLEETOne campaign brings together five to fifty new smart accounts for one operator.
POLICYIts permitted activity, budget, and expiry are set before sponsorship begins.
RECOVERYOwner credentials and recovery material stay encrypted locally.

02 / privacy boundary

Public activity, deliberately incomplete attribution.

Accounts, trades, amounts, timing, gas, and shared-paymaster activity remain visible on the public record. Chit does not publish the primary-wallet-to-fleet relationship.

PUBLIC RECORD

Each account and its sponsored activity remain available to observe.

PRIVATE EDGE

The relationship between the primary wallet and fleet is not published. In MVP, the operator knows the mapping.

03 / control model

A budget with a stopping point.

  • PAUSEStops new sponsored execution until the operator resumes.
  • REVOKEEnds sponsorship for that campaign; closing remains available.
  • CLOSEReturns unused campaign gas budget to its owner.

04 / development truth

The line is being built with its limits in view.

Chit is in development for private beta. This page describes the intended boundary and control model, not a live service.

05 / closing note

The public record can stay honest without publishing every connection.
