Chit is building private gas sponsorship for a single operator working through 5–50 newly generated programmable smart accounts.

Read the boundary ↓

01 / mechanism

A campaign draws the limits before it sponsors anything.

One campaign holds its permitted activity, gas budget, and expiry. New accounts are created for that campaign; local owner credentials remain locally encrypted.

02 / privacy boundary

What is visible is not the whole relationship.

VISIBLE

Public accounts, trades, amounts, timing, gas, and shared-paymaster activity remain visible.

NOT PUBLISHED

The operator-to-fleet relationship is not published. In MVP, the operator knows that mapping.

03 / control and risk

A sponsorship line must be easy to stop.

  • PAUSEBlocks new sponsored execution until a campaign is resumed.
  • REVOKEEnds sponsorship for the campaign; continuing later requires a new one.
  • CLOSEReturns unused campaign gas budget to its owner.

04 / development truth

Private beta is being shaped with the boundary intact.

This is an intended product model, still in development. It does not describe hidden trades, a live network, or a complete privacy guarantee.

05 / closing action

The line can remain private without pretending the activity disappears.
