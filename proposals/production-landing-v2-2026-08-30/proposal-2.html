04 / control and risk

Control and risk

PAUSE

Stops new sponsorship until the operator resumes the campaign.

REVOKE

Ends sponsorship for that campaign. It does not resume.

CLOSE

Returns unused campaign gas budget to the campaign owner.

05 / development truth
