Fleet / private gas sponsorship
Product brief / in development

Private attribution for a visible trading fleet.

Chit is building sponsorship for gas across 5–50 trading-wallet accounts—without publishing the line between a primary wallet and its fleet.

READING NOTEThis is a public product brief. It does not open a fleet, collect a waitlist, or imply live availability.

01 / SCOPE LEDGER

The edge is private. The activity is not.

Visible record

Public accounts, trades, amounts, timing, gas, and shared-paymaster activity remain on the public record.

Private edge

The primary-wallet-to-fleet mapping is not published by the product.

MVP reality

The operator knows the mapping. Chit is not claiming anonymous activity, hidden trades, or fleet-wide unlinkability.

What a careful fleet requires.

01

One trader-controlled campaign, five to fifty newly generated accounts, and a bounded sponsorship budget.

02

Approved activity, a maximum value, gas limits, and an expiry define the permitted sponsorship line.

03

Local encrypted credentials and a downloaded recovery record stay with the trader.

04

Pause, revocation, depletion, expiry, and closure stop further sponsored activity.
